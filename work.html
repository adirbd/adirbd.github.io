Infrastructure, end to end

From on-prem server rooms to the cloud: DevOps, systems integration, team leadership, and networking. What I care about most is reliability and follow-through, the parts that show up long after the launch.

Today I work as a DevOps Engineer at Check Point on the cloud side, doing secure infrastructure, automation, CI/CD, and repeatable delivery. Before that I spent years deep in on-prem infrastructure: building and integrating complex environments with servers, networking, firewalls, and virtualization, and leading the team that ran them. Having worked both sides, physical data centers and the cloud, shapes how I think about systems.

What I focus on

  • Secure foundations. Cloud infrastructure, compliance-minded changes, and systems that stand up to review.
  • Delivery workflows. CI/CD, reusable templates, and release paths teams can trust and repeat.
  • Both sides. On-prem infrastructure and cloud, networking, and the reality that follows deployment.
