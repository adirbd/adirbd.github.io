About

Work, curiosity, and the systems behind both

I’m interested in reliable technology, clear thinking, and how large systems actually behave over time.

Professionally, I work in DevOps and infrastructure. Personally, I’m drawn to transportation, urban systems, aviation, and public projects where engineering, economics, and long-term usefulness meet.

Work lens Infrastructure, automation, and decisions that stay practical after launch.
Outside work Transit, cities, aviation, and places that make systems feel human again.

What matters to me in engineering

I prefer systems that are understandable, resilient, and practical to operate. Good tooling matters, but so do tradeoffs, ownership, and how a system behaves once real people depend on it.

What pulls me outside of work

I keep coming back to infrastructure in the broader sense: rail, metro, aviation, streets, public return on investment, and why some systems age into trust while others age into friction.

The personal layer

Coffee, trying different beers out of curiosity, trips that reset perspective, and movement that clears the head — sometimes snowboarding, sometimes just getting out of routine.
